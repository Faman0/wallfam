About us

Every wallpaper is an invitation to discover a new landscape and open your home to the extraordinary.

Wallfam is a brushstroke of beauty

Every painting in the Wallfam collection is a ray of light shed on our need for beauty, reflected in the mirror of nature. Magical landscapes open our interiors to the unexpected and turn our everyday rooms into places where we want to spend time.

A wallpaper or a painting — depending on your preference — will open the four walls of your home to beauty. By transferring hand-painted paintings onto high-quality non-woven fabric, we can offer you original art in your home.

Every Wallfam artistic wallpaper is made to the measurements of a specific wall. The base design format is 500×300 cm, adapted to the dimensions provided, taking niches and sloped ceilings into account; lead time ~2 weeks.
