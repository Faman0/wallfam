Wallpapers in the Lascaux collection

The Lascaux collection was inspired by the prehistoric cave paintings of the Lascaux cave in France, where thousands of years ago someone pressed a hand to a wall to leave a mark on it. The non-woven fabric with a fresco texture makes the image seem to grow into the wall. The palette is airy. Whitened greens, smoky blues, warm ochres. Over a dozen hand-painted botanical motifs, each available as a wallpaper with the original texture.

Lascaux - Feathered Hues

    Price: EUR 100/m2

    Our paintings and wallpapers are originally designed at a size of 500x300 cm.
    To achieve the most artistic effect possible, the image is scaled to your room and wall.

    When ordering, please provide the name of the wallpaper, the collection, and the dimensions of the wall or stucco mouldings.
    Based on the information provided, we prepare a wallpaper design with details of the lead time and the total price.

    The standard lead time is approximately two weeks from the moment the order is placed.
    The wallpapers are printed on non-woven fabric. In the Lascaux collection, the material has a special texture that accentuates the fresco effect.
