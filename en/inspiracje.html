Your wall is waiting for a story

Every wall is a question. Before the wallpaper went up, before you chose the colour of the paint — the wall was asking what you are really looking for. Room to breathe? A deep forest that knows no boundaries of its room? A morning bird that sings regardless of the weather outside the window.

The inspirations gathered here are for those who, instead of asking what to hang on the wall, ask themselves a different question: what do I want to listen to every morning as I cross the threshold of my room?

Which wall to choose

Not every wall needs a painting. But every room has one that is waiting.

Usually it is the one opposite the entrance — the first you see when you walk in. Interior designers call it an accent wall, but that is too technical a word for something that works more like a stage. When you enter a room and see a forest, water, a blossoming garden — the rest of the space automatically stops being a “room” and becomes a place.

A made-to-measure artistic wallpaper does not need symmetry. It does not have to fill the whole room. One well-chosen wall is enough — the power of concentration does the rest.

Perhaps even the one at the end of the hallway, the one behind the bed, the one behind the desk — a single surface changes the whole space. Not because it is everywhere. Because it is exactly where calm should be.

Where an artistic wallpaper lives most fully

Different rooms follow different rules. Here is how to think about your choice depending on the space — and what to look for in each.

Living room

A space that talks to your guests before you have said a word. The deep Arles collection — with its saturated colour and distinct brushstroke — is exceptionally well suited here. A living-room wallpaper should be the wall at which people rest their eyes. And return to it.

Bedroom

What matters here is what you see first on waking. The gentler texture of the Lascaux collection, with its fresco effect and whitened tones, creates a kind of visual silence in the bedroom. It makes no noise. It keeps you company. A bedroom wallpaper should work like a landscape that does not demand attention — but is there when you look for it.

Study

Nature is the oldest antidote to overload. Research in biophilic design shows that the presence of plant and water motifs lowers cortisol levels and improves concentration. A wallpaper with a botanical motif behind the desk is not decoration — it is a tool.

Dining room

We rarely think of the dining room as an artistic space, and that is a mistake. Meals last. Conversations at the table last. An artistic wallpaper in the dining room becomes the backdrop to everyday stories — and is looked at for a long time, up close, in good light. View of the Canal, Maidens in Pompeii — a motif chosen for the dining room works differently than anywhere else.

Lascaux or Arles — two moods, one story

Both collections begin in the same place: with a hand-painted painting that the artist created on canvas. The difference lies in how that painting is transferred to the wall — and what it breathes in its new surroundings.

Lascaux Collection

For those who seek silence. Non-woven fabric with a fresco texture evokes prehistoric cave paintings — not through the motif, but through a kind of presence. The colours are airy: whitened greens, smoky blues, warm ochres. Nothing shouts. Everything simply is.

It suits bright, Scandinavian and classic interiors — anywhere you want more air than drama.

Arles Collection

For those who like a wall that has an opinion. The painter's canvas texture emphasises every brushstroke, and the colours are saturated and bold. Arles echoes what Van Gogh saw in the Provençal light — intensity, vibration, colour as emotion in itself.

For living rooms with character, studies that are meant to inspire, bedrooms in which you want to sleep wrapped in depth rather than delicacy.

How a wallpaper changes throughout the day

One of the most rarely described aspects of made-to-measure artistic wallpapers is that they are alive. Not metaphorically — they literally change with the light.

In the morning, in side light through the window, the fresco texture of the Lascaux collection casts shadows between the brush marks. The image seems three-dimensional, as if painted on plaster. At noon, in overhead light, the same colours become flat and clean. In the evening, in warm lamplight, the botanical motif — leaves, branches, flowers — comes alive with a depth that was not there in the morning.

Under artificial light, the Arles collection becomes even warmer. Sunny Salute in the evening does not look like a wallpaper — it looks like a keepsake of an afternoon that is still going.

When choosing an artistic wallpaper, it is worth seeing it at different times of day. That is why we offer the option of ordering a sample before you make your final decision — write to us and we will send a material sample from your chosen collection.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about made-to-measure artistic wallpapers and original Wallfam paintings.

Is a hand-painted wallpaper durable?
Wallfam wallpapers are printed on non-woven fabric — a more durable base than paper wallpapers, which does not need soaking when hung. The Lascaux collection has a texture imitating fresco, the Arles collection a painter's canvas texture. Both materials are fade-resistant and suitable for living spaces with normal humidity.
Can an artistic wallpaper be ordered in a non-standard size?
Yes — every Wallfam wallpaper is made to the measurements of a specific wall. The base design format is 500×300 cm, but the image is scaled to the dimensions provided by the customer, taking niches, doors and sloped ceilings into account. Just give the dimensions when placing your order.
How much does a made-to-measure artistic wallpaper cost?
Wallfam wallpapers cost PLN 380/m². The final quote depends on the dimensions of the wall and is prepared individually — free of charge. Contact us with the dimensions of your wall and we will come back with a quote and a design proposal.
How long does it take to make a custom wallpaper?
The standard lead time is about two weeks from the moment the order is placed and the design approved.
Can I order a painting from the same collection as the wallpaper?
Yes — every motif is available both as a made-to-measure wallpaper and as an original hand-painted artwork. You can order both at the same time or separately. This is an offer unique on the Polish market — the chance to own the wallpaper and the original from which it was created.
How do the Lascaux and Arles collections differ?
Lascaux has a fresco texture and a light, airy palette — ideal for bedrooms, Scandinavian and classic interiors. Arles has a painter's canvas texture and deep, saturated colours — for living rooms and spaces with a distinct character. The botanical motifs are identical in both collections — what sets them apart is the mood and the material.
