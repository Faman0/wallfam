How to place an order

We fulfil every order individually, with care for detail, material and the character of the space in which our works will live.

How to order a painting

To order a painting, choose the composition from our collection that interests you and the version (1, 2 or 3) in which it is to be made.

Include this information — the name of the painting and the version number — directly in your email. On this basis we will prepare your order and let you know the lead time.

wallfam@wallfam.eu →

How to order a wallpaper

To order a wallpaper, measure the wall on which it is to go and decide which pattern, in which collection, interests you.

In your email, give the dimensions of the wall, the name of the chosen wallpaper pattern and the name of the collection (Lascaux or Arles). On this basis we will prepare a quote.

wallfam@wallfam.eu →
