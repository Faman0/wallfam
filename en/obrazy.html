Paintings

Paintings are the foundation for creating the wallpaper collections. Creative exploration finds its first results on canvas. The white of the void slowly fills with colours from which an image emerges. In the same way, an empty wall, thanks to a painting or a wallpaper, can become the beginning of a work of art.
